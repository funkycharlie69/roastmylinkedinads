### Critical "Pro" Advice for the Build:

1.  **The Price Point:** Since you are selling "Expertise" and removing the "Agency" bias, do not underprice this.
    *   If you charge **$200**, you look like a freelancer.
    *   If you charge **$900 - $1,500**, you look like a Specialist Consultant.
    *   *Recommendation:* Start at **$495** or **$795** as an "Early Bird" offer since you are launching, then raise it.

2.  **The "Viewer" Access Detail:** This is your friction killer. Emphasize heavily that they are **NOT** giving you Admin rights. This removes the fear of "What if he messes up my campaigns?"

3.  **Stripe Redirect:**
    *   Set up your Stripe product to redirect *immediately* to a "Thank You / Onboarding" page.
    *   **Onboarding Page Copy:** "Payment Received. Here is the exact email address to add to your LinkedIn Ad Account. As soon as I get the LinkedIn notification, your 48h timer starts."

4.  **Video Intro:**
    *   Record a 45-second video for the Hero section. Just you, looking at the camera.
    *   *Script:* "Hi, I'm Charles. I built CAPAC. I don't want to run your ads. But I see companies burning thousands every day on bad settings. Send me your account, give me 48 hours, and I'll tell you exactly what I'd do if it were my money. No fluff, just the fix."
