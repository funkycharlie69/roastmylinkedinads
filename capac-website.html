FAQs

Answers to questions you might have about CAPAC.

I already use tools for LinkedIn ads, do I still need CAPAC?

Absolutely. No other tool helps with manual bidding.

CAPAC layers on top of your existing stack, finds hidden overspend or underspend, and fixes bids (soon automatically).

Is CAPAC setup complicated?

Two clicks onboarding - done.

Onboarding flow:
1- Click “Sign in with LinkedIn” – secure SSO, no forms.
2- Click “Authorize” – gives CAPAC access to your ad accounts.

Using CAPAC:
- See all you ad accounts ordered by spend.
- See all your active campaigns.
- Analyse, optimize or schedule.

No tags, no spreadsheets, no dashboard building, just click, optimise, repeat.
You’re done optimising campaigns before the coffee’s brewed.

Can I connect unlimited client accounts?

Yes. Every paid plan includes unlimited ad accounts and campaigns.
‍
Why that matters:
- Zero hidden fees. Whether you handle 5 accounts or 200, your price stays the same.
- Jump between ad accounts with one click.
- Landed a new client today? Their account is available on CAPAC the moment you get access to their Linkedin ad account. No action required on your end.

Unlimited really means unlimited.

How does the money‑back guarantee work if I cancel?

One click = full refund.

The exact steps:
- Within the first 30 days, go to Billing › Cancel & Refund inside your CAPAC account.
- Click “Refund”. That’s it. Stripe instantly returns 100 % of what you paid.
‍
No ticket, no email thread, no pro‑rated trickery.
‍
‍What happens next:
- Your plan changes to the FREE Tier = You still have access to the the tools for 1 ad account.
- For all your other campaigns, we store your data (bid change history) for 30 days, but we stop creating new data. After 30 days, your data is deleted.

If you ever return, you can reactivate in one click.

‍Bottom line: try CAPAC for a full 30 days with zero financial risk. If it’s not a fit, you get every dollar back, guaranteed.

How secure is my data?

Security is one of our top priorities.
‍
CAPAC only stores the data necessary to provide our service. Your data is end-to-end encrypted, on secure data centres in Paris, France.

Our database complies with security and privacy standards, such as GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2, ensuring that its security practices meet industry standards.

When will the fully automated “Bidding  Autopilot” launch?

The tool is currently in Private Beta.

Timeline:
- Q 3 2025 — private beta with a shortlist of users (Reserve a spot here →)
- Q 4 2025 — public rollout.

‍How it works:
- You set guardrails: % thresholds, min/max bids, max increase/decrease.
- CAPAC analyses campaigns 24/7 and auto‑applies bid changes.
- Weekly emails shows what changed and the before/after performance.
